About the data

Powered by NOAA & the National Weather Service

Current conditions come from NWS observation station KSUN (Friedman Memorial Airport, Hailey). Hourly and 7-day forecasts are generated by the NWS forecast office in Pocatello (PIH) for the Sun Valley gridpoint. Data is refreshed automatically when you load the page.
